AI Governance in transition — from setting policy to operational management

From Policy
Governance before deployment
Policies
Frameworks
Risk assessments
Documentation and records
It works for chatbots. It is not enough for AI agents.
To Operations
AI governance at runtime
See what is active, in real time
Know access to tools, data and workflows
Trace actions, pipelines and data consumed
Monitor outputs, cost and failure patterns
Intervene during operation
Produce evidence of conformity in real time
This is AI Governance infrastructure.
The next level of AI governance: make agents operational, secure evidence and auditability, assign responsibilities, and ultimately build trust.
